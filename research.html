Residential Mold and Mycotoxin Contamination: A Cost-Centered Literature Review for Managerial Decision-Making

Literature review submitted in partial fulfillment of Doctor of Business Administration requirements · Sources 2015–2025

Residential mold contamination is a widespread environmental-health and economic problem, with direct and indirect U.S. costs estimated in the review at $3.5 billion to $10 billion a year. Despite growing awareness of mycotoxin exposure risk and widespread property damage, the decisions homeowners, property managers and insurers actually make are fragmented, reactive and economically suboptimal.

The review synthesizes more than 100 peer-reviewed sources, industry standards, insurance guidelines and regulatory documents into a cost-centered framework for understanding how those parties decide under scientific uncertainty, information asymmetry and resource constraints.
